💊 About TB Treatment

  • Treatment lasts 6 months (180 days) for drug-sensitive TB
  • Pills must be taken every day at the same time
  • Never skip a dose — TB can become resistant
  • Missing doses can make treatment fail

⚠️ Common Side Effects

  • Nausea or upset stomach
  • Loss of appetite
  • Mild skin rash
  • Orange/red urine (from rifampicin — normal!)
  • Tiredness
💡 Tip: Give pills with food to reduce stomach upset

🚨 When to Seek Urgent Care

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe vomiting (can't keep pills down)
  • Yellowing of eyes or skin (jaundice)
  • Severe rash or swelling
  • Confusion or severe headache
🚨 Call your clinic immediately if any of these occur!

🤝 Supporting Your Child

  • Praise every dose taken — celebrate small wins
  • Use the PhiliGo app together during pill time
  • Keep a calm, reassuring tone
  • Link pill time to a daily routine (e.g., after breakfast)
  • Never punish for missed doses — encourage instead
