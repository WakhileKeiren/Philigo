💊 Eswatini STG & EML (2021) Care Principles

  • Complete the full prescribed duration (e.g., 6 months / 180 days for standard TB therapy) even when feeling better.
  • Daily adherence prevents drug resistance and relapse.
  • Cross-check weight-based paediatric dosing with the Eswatini Paediatric Standard Treatment Guidelines at each clinic review.
  • Pair pharmacotherapy with non-pharmacological support: balanced nutrition, clean water, ventilation, and trigger avoidance (for asthma/inhalers).

💨 Visual Dosage & Inhaler Technique

  • Tablets / Dispersible Tablets: Dissolve dispersible paediatric tablets in clean water as instructed.
  • Syrups: Shake well and measure with an oral syringe, never a household spoon.
  • Inhalers + Spacer: Shake inhaler, seal lips around spacer mouthpiece, actuate once, and take 5 slow breaths.
  • Expected harmless effect (Rifampicin): Orange/red urine, tears, or sweat is normal.
💡 Give medicines at consistent daily anchor times (breakfast, after school, bedtime).

🚨 Danger Signs — Refer Immediately

  • Fast or difficult breathing, chest indrawing, or blue lips
  • Persistent vomiting (unable to keep medicines down)
  • Yellowing of eyes or skin (jaundice) or dark tea-colored urine
  • Severe skin rash, facial swelling, or vision changes
  • Lethargy, confusion, or severe headache
🚨 Visit your nearest Eswatini clinic or hospital immediately if any danger sign appears!
