🔔 Alerts

💊 About TB Treatment

  • TB treatment lasts 6 months (180 days)
  • Pills must be taken every single day
  • Never skip doses — TB can become resistant
  • Missing doses can cause treatment failure

⚠️ Common Side Effects

  • Nausea or upset stomach
  • Loss of appetite
  • Mild skin rash
  • Orange/red urine (normal — from rifampicin)
  • Tiredness
💡 Give pills with food to reduce stomach upset

🚨 Seek Urgent Care

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe vomiting (can't keep pills down)
  • Yellowing of eyes or skin (jaundice)
  • Severe rash or swelling
  • Confusion or severe headache
🚨 Call your clinic immediately if any occur!

🤝 Supporting Your Child

  • Praise every dose taken — celebrate wins
  • Play PhiliGo together during pill time
  • Link pill time to a daily routine
  • Never punish for missed doses — encourage
